## What a user sees
With Ruler on in page view, a narrow ruler left of every page with inch numbers down it and the top/bottom margins shaded.
Drag the top marker down (or the bottom marker up) to widen the margins; the top ruler's markers move with it. Up/Down arrows change by 6 px (Shift 24), Home 24, End 192.
Hidden with Ruler off, in continuous view, in focus mode, and while the sidebar floats.

## Plan versus code
- `SideRuler` props: the plan lists `onDragStart(event)` and `onKeyDown(event)` only. The component has two markers and needs move/up/cancel handlers
  (pointer capture is on the marker), so the props are `onDragStart(event, edge)`, `onDragMove`, `onDragEnd`, `onDragCancel`, `onKeyDown(event, edge)`.
- The 16 px left of the paper is the viewport's 16 px padding (`.is-paged` padding), so the stop rule did not trigger; the paper edge needs no shift.
  At 150 percent zoom the strip is 24 screen px wide and the viewport's `overflow-x: clip` may cut its outer 8 px (inferred; the test checks alignment, not visibility of that part).
- `grep` showed `paperGeometry` lives in `src/editor/formatting.ts`, not a `pageLayout` file.
