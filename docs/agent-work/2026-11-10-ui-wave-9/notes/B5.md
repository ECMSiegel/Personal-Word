# B5 -- Blank-page click places the caret

## What changed
- `src/editor/blankClick.ts`: `blankPageClick(view, event)` now handles a primary-button click that
  lands on the paper but outside the text. It returns false (leaves the click alone) for non-primary
  buttons, ctrl/meta/alt, any descendant of `view.dom` (other than `view.dom` itself), and anything
  inside `.document-ruler`, `.side-ruler`, `.page-spacer`, `[data-footnote-id]`, or
  `button, a, input, textarea, select, [role="slider"]`. Otherwise: below the last child of
  `view.dom` -> document end; else `posAtCoords` with x/y clamped inside the writing surface (null ->
  document end). It dispatches a `TextSelection` (shift extends from the current anchor) with
  `scrollIntoView()`, calls `preventDefault()` and `view.focus()`.
- `src/editor/blankClick.test.ts`: 15 unit tests of the decision logic with fake target chains.
- `tests/w9-blank-click.spec.ts`: continuous and page view, three short paragraphs, cases (a) to (e).
- Test fix this round: the old (e) clicked inside text while the (d) shift-selection (4..13) was
  still active. A press inside an existing selection is the browser's drag-start path and settles on
  mouse-up, so about 1 run in 5 read the old head (13) before it settled. (e) now collapses the
  selection first and waits (`expect.poll`) for the caret to move. No assertion was loosened, no
  sleeps. Product code was not changed for this.

## What a user sees
Clicking the grey paper area beside or below the text (left/right margin, below the last line) puts
the blinking caret at the nearest sensible spot: margin click -> start/end of the line level with the
click, below the text -> end of the document. Shift-click in the margin extends the selection.
Clicks on the ruler, footnote markers, page gaps and inside text behave as before.

## Limitations
- Chromium only; not checked in native WebKit.
- Split-pane case (blank click in unfocused pane moving the toolbar) not covered: pane focus is
  routed in `src/App.tsx`, which this task does not own.

## Hand check (built app, at 100% and 150% zoom)
1. Type three short paragraphs. 2. Click on the paper well below the last line: caret at the end,
typing appends. 3. Click in the left margin level with paragraph 2: caret at its start. 4. Same in
the right margin: caret at its end. 5. Select a few words, Shift+click in the margin: selection
extends. 6. Click on the ruler, a footnote marker, a page gap and inside text: each does what it did
before.
