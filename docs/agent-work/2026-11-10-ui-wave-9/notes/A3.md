# A3 -- Card view polish (T257)

## What changed
- `src/styles.css` (card view only, all under `.cards-view`):
  - Cards and their content can shrink to the list width (`min-width: 0`, `max-width: 100%`); titles truncate with an ellipsis on one line.
  - A PDF child card steps in one `--space-4` from its source card (margin on the card, label offset kept at the same place).
  - Research cards: one column (the two-column rule at 380 px now excludes `data-group="research"`), no preview block, vertical padding `--space-2` so each card is 40 px or less.
  - The research open-beside icon sits inside the card (`right: var(--space-2)`); it used to sit 7 px past the edge and made the group scroll sideways.
  - `.cards-view .reorder` (open-beside for entries): `top` and `right` at `--space-3`, no extra button padding, so the icon is inset from the card's corner.
  - Empty card boxes: a card with no synopsis and no draft status hides its Synopsis input and status row at rest and on hover; they show once the card is open or keyboard focus is inside it.
- `src/components/ProjectSidebar.tsx`: one attribute, `data-empty` on the manuscript and fragment entry rows (`!e.synopsis && !e.draftStatus`). The scroll-fade hook from A2 is untouched.
- `tests/w9-cards.spec.ts` (new): 3 width tests (220, 254, 420 px) and 1 empty-box test.

## What a user sees
- Card view no longer runs past the left or right edge of the sidebar at narrow widths, and long titles end with an ellipsis.
- Research items are single-line rows in one column, with the Bibliography row at the normal row height.
- The split-pane icon on a card sits inside the card's top-right corner.
- A card with no synopsis shows only its title until it is opened or focused; a card with a synopsis always shows its synopsis and status.

## Interpretation (please check)
- "Notes row" in the plan is taken to mean the entry's draft-status row (the "Notes / Draft / Revised" dropdown). The model has no separate notes field, so an empty card means: no synopsis and no draft status set.
