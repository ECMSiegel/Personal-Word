# B4 -- Open beside replaces the pane last used

Task T261, agent opencode-watch. Plan: docs/agent-work/2026-11-10-ui-wave-9/PLAN.md section "### B4 --".

## What changed

- `src/split.ts` `openBeside`: when a split is open and the target is an entry,
  it now returns `select(split, target.id)` -- the entry replaces the focused
  pane (the pane last used), and focus follows the entry when it is already
  shown in the other pane. With no split open, and for PDF targets, the
  previous body is unchanged (entry beside the active one in a new right pane;
  PDF always in the right pane, focus stays left). Signature, callers
  (`src/App.tsx:575`, `:955`) and the doc comment updated (comment only; no
  caller edited).
- `src/split.test.ts`: five new/updated cases in `describe("openBeside")` --
  (a) no split, entry target (unchanged result), (b) split focused left, entry
  `c` -> left becomes `c`, right unchanged, focus left, (c) split focused
  right, entry `c` -> right becomes `c`, (d) target already shown in the other
  pane -> no pane changes, focus moves to that pane, (e) PDF target -> right
  pane regardless of focus.
- `tests/w9-open-beside.spec.ts` (new): the browser flow for left-focused
  replace, right-focused replace, focus-moves case, and the unchanged no-split
  case.

## Old expectations re-pinned (plan's authorised default)

Two existing tests in `tests/split-view.spec.ts` and one in
`src/split.test.ts` encoded the old rule ("Open beside always replaces the
right pane while the left pane keeps its entry"). Re-pinned per the plan's
"Default if unanswered":

1. `src/split.test.ts`, old test "replaces the right pane of an open split"
   quoted: with `{left:"a", right:b, focused:"left"}` and target `c` it
   expected `{left:"a", right:c, focused:"right"}`. Now: left becomes `c`,
   focus stays left (new test "replaces the left pane when it is focused").
2. `src/split.test.ts`, old test "does nothing for the entry already on the
   left" quoted: `openBeside(pair, "b", {kind:"entry", id:"a"})` expected `toBe(pair)`
   (no change). Now: focus moves to the pane already showing the target
   (plan case (d); new test "focuses the pane already showing the target").
3. `tests/split-view.spec.ts:419-427` "open beside: the row button ...":
   after the "Open One beside" step the old rule kept focus on the right
   pane, so the final "Open Two beside" click replaced the right pane
   (assertion at :427 expected right title "Two") and the test then deleted
   section Two from the right pane. Under B4 the One step moves focus to the
   left pane, so Two lands in the left pane. Re-pinned by focusing the right
   pane before the final open-beside click, which restores the test's intent
   (delete the right pane's entry closes the split) without weakening any
   assertion. The comment at :419 now records the B4 focus move.

## What a user sees

With a split open, a sidebar "Open beside" click (or Cmd/Ctrl+click, or Open
in editor) now replaces the pane the user was last working in, instead of
always replacing the right pane. If the entry is already open in the other
pane, the focus just moves to it. With no split open nothing changes: the
entry opens in a new right pane and the right pane takes focus.

## Hand check for the note (owner, built app)

1. Open a project with three sections. Open section One; Cmd/Ctrl+click Two
   (split opens, right pane focused). Click into the left pane, then press a
   third entry's Open beside button: the LEFT pane shows it, the right pane
   still shows Two, and the breadcrumb shows the third entry.
2. Same split, click into the right pane, press section One's Open beside:
   the right pane shows One, the left pane keeps its entry.
3. With One on the left and focus on the right, press One's Open beside
   again: no pane swaps; focus moves to the left pane (breadcrumb One).
4. Close the split; press an entry's Open beside: it opens in a new right
   pane as before and the right pane takes focus.
