# Limitations and resolved issues

Part 1 lists what is still true today. Part 2 lists issues that were fixed, newest first. Evidence for the figures is in the [validation record](validation.md).

## Part 1 -- Current limitations

### (a) Deliberate design limits

- **Page breaks differ between views.** The editor's page view, PDF export, and Word export each lay out pages differently. Expect page breaks to move; exact page parity is not promised. **Preview PDF** shows the same PDF exporter's output. An exact print-engine preview does not exist.
- **Page view splits only plain paragraphs.** Page view stacks pages vertically and splits a paragraph between lines as Word does by default: at least two lines stay on each page, so a paragraph of three lines or fewer moves whole, and a heading moves with the paragraph after it when that paragraph moves whole. Lists, block quotes, tables, images and code blocks are not split: one that does not fit moves whole to the next page (a page can end with blank space up to that block's height), and one taller than a whole page starts a page and runs straight across the page gap and margins. In justified text, the last line of a paragraph's part on a page is not stretched to the full width. Moving the caret Up or Down across a page gap lands on the line across the gap but forgets the column it started from. Where a paragraph breaks across pages, the caret cannot sit after the last character of page one's last line (that position is drawn at the top of the next page), so clicking in that page's bottom margin puts it one character before the line's end. Every page reserves the same footnote space, sized for the page with the most notes. A pane narrower than the page shows the page scaled down, not reflowed. Word and PDF exports keep their own pagination, which does split paragraphs between lines.
- **PDF uses bundled fonts.** PDF export draws with bundled Noto fonts, not the fonts on your Mac. It covers Latin, Greek, and Cyrillic scripts. Other scripts are refused with a message, so use Word export for them. A footnote too long to fit on one page is also refused rather than cut off. Word continuation paragraphs of a multi-paragraph footnote have no indent while PDF's are indented.
- **Citation help covers common sources.** Books, journal articles, and websites are supported. The current development source also formats bounded examples for print edited-book chapters, ProQuest PhD dissertations, signed online newspaper articles, broadcast audio interviews and online TED Talks. Those examples have browser-preview and generated-export checks; they do not cover every subtype. Other imported records retain raw fields and offer manual citation forms. Full audio/video notes with specific time references currently require manual text. Lookup results can be wrong or incomplete. Check every citation and your instructor's rules yourself. By default, short notes run across the manuscript and never use ibid. An opt-in **Use repeat citation rules** setting in the Research panel lets you choose to show the full citation again once per section and to use "Ibid." for back-to-back repeats instead of a short note (`src/research/ResearchPanel.tsx:686`).
- **Version comparison works by paragraph.** It does not track changes word by word.
- **Fonts depend on your Mac.** The interface prefers Futura, then Avenir. No commercial fonts are included.
- **Saving is tested, not guaranteed.** Personal Word uses SQLite WAL with macOS `fullfsync=ON` and immediate transactions. Isolated native tests killed the app at checkpoints between asset staging and save, between the database commit and the portable copies, during a research-source merge and during a snapshot restore, and the project came back intact each time. On this Intel Mac a 100,000-word one-section save measured 116.57 / 130.43 / 124.37 ms at the 95th percentile at low load, and 141.69 ms on 2026-10-25 at moderate load (load average about 20-26) after the cheap-fixes changes (budget 150 ms; earlier runs on a heavily loaded Mac took 347 to 499 ms). Apple silicon is unmeasured. Sudden power loss, a kernel panic or disk failure cannot be guaranteed against; keep external backups.
- **Zotero refresh scope.** The optional refresh is off by default, read-only, and limited to your own Mac (127.0.0.1:23119); every change goes through the review dialog. It works in the desktop app only; the browser preview shows a desktop-only message and makes no network read. It has no distinct "Zotero is off" message and has never been run against a real Zotero. Updates to a matched source preserve identity and linked notes, leave unselected local fields intact, and require a checked safety snapshot. No fields are selected automatically; replacing original CSL data replaces that whole field.
- **Imports have size caps and cannot carry everything.** Plain text and Markdown files (up to 5 MB) keep their structure, but images, tables and raw HTML inside them arrive as visible literal text with a warning. HTML, Word (.docx) and Markdown-with-images (.zip) bundle files (up to 20 MB) import headings, lists, links, notes, rectangular tables and PNG/JPEG images through a preview dialog. They check the zip's declared sizes before unpacking, parse HTML without running it, and never fetch an outside image (it is omitted with a warning). Cancel creates nothing. A bundle's checksums are verified on import. Merged or nested table cells are not supported, and the app has no live multi-user collaboration or tracked changes. **Cancel import** discards the result of a slow parse and creates nothing; abandoning the dialog with Escape (while reading or at the preview) is covered by browser tests (2026-10-25), but abandoning it by leaving the screen is not. The XML check refuses only external DOCTYPE declarations.
- **Writing aids are optional heuristics.** Prose checks can be wrong; they never correct text. Goals count manuscript sections only, and daily baselines reset by the local date. When a section has an extreme number of flagged words, only the issues nearest the cursor are shown (the panel says so) so typing stays responsive.
- **Native title bar follows the in-app theme only in the Mac app.** The choice of Light or Dark is passed to the window title bar through Tauri; browser builds do nothing, and only the Mac app shows whether it matches.
- **Drag-and-drop import is checked in the browser, not by hand on the Mac.** The Mac path (Tauri drop event, the dropped-file read command, the highlight in WebKit) has native suite runs but no hand drag from Finder yet; the owner check is pending.
- **Split view is global in ratio and per project in layout.** The divider ratio is remembered globally; which entry is open beside is remembered per project. The left editor is never remounted, so opening or closing a split keeps its undo history and scroll position, and the footer page number follows the focused pane. The divider's hit area is 6 px wide so it can be grabbed, wider than the 1 px line it draws.
- **Research PDFs stay in the project folder.** A PDF attached to a source or added with Upload PDF (25 MB cap) is stored with the project and drawn locally; it is not carried inside the project's `.json` archive or the Markdown bundle, so a project moved that way loses the attachment even though the bytes stay in the original project.
- **Counters use proportional digits.** The format bar is icons only at every width, so there is no width threshold to tune. The footer page counter and the save status ask for tabular figures, but Futura has no tabular digits: widths stay proportional (111 and 888 measured 18.719 px and 22.219 px), so a changing number can shift by a pixel or two. The fallback font decides; no font is substituted to avoid it.
- **Native screenshots are 1x.** The native capture tool saves 1240x840 pixels, soft on Retina displays; the open create panel is not captured natively.
- **Wave 4 was checked in Chromium only.** The UI wave 4 changes (page sheets, text emphasis, dock, split footers, sidebar selection, focus-mode bar) have browser and unit evidence; native WebKit has not been run for them. The page-sheet change in particular targets a WebKit-only drawing bug, so only a look in the Mac app at zoom 50, 75, 125 and 150 confirms it.
- **The faux bold is tuned in Chromium, not in Futura.** Hover and focus on text controls use a text stroke (`--emph-stroke`, 0.04em) that was matched to a true semibold in Chromium's fallback font. How it looks in Futura in the Mac app is to be confirmed; if it reads too light or heavy, change the one token. The stroke never changes widths, but the 4 percent grow is visual and can lightly touch a very close neighbour.
- **A revealed focus-mode bar covers text that is scrolled under it.** At the top of the document the reserved band keeps the first line clear, but once you scroll, text passes under the bar while it is shown (it hides again 600 ms after the pointer leaves). Printing the live editor in focus mode may keep the extra top band; the exporters are unaffected.
- **Page sheets appear one frame after the page count changes.** Sheets are drawn from React state set from the pagination plugin's page count, so a new page's spacer can exist for one frame before its sheet (the same lag the page-count minimum height always had).
- **Backspace/Delete at a page break does nothing inside a wrapper.** If the paragraph next to the break is the first or last child of a block quote or list item, `deleteAcrossBreak` finds no neighbour and ProseMirror's default applies (it selects the break node), as before wave 4.
- **Motion spec (e) had a timing race, not an app defect.** The test pressed Escape 40 ms into the 180 ms show, so the reversed hide could finish before the reopen and unmount the menu legitimately; it reproduces on HEAD. Fixed in the test on 2026-11-02 (Escape at 110 ms plus an `isConnected` precondition).
- **Focus mode keeps the shared bar in split view.** Only a single pane hides its formatting bar; in split view the shared bar stays visible so the panes never jump. The native print probe (smoke build only) is the one path that reads the live editor, so the focus-mode top band cannot reach a real export.
- **A block taller than a page still runs across the page gap.** In page view a table, image or list taller than a whole page starts a page and crosses the gap and margins; the sheets and spacers are correct but the block is drawn over the gap. This is the same documented limit as before (see the page-splitting note above).
- **Destination pages light nothing in the sidebar sections.** While Settings, Comments, Bibliography or Read is on screen no section header or entry is bold (wave 3 kept the entry bold there). Flagged for the owner's hand check.
- **Read mode has two scroll rails doing the same job.** The sidebar's scrollspy lines and the older rail at the right edge of the Read view both show where you are; the old rail is kept by default for now. In Read mode a click on a sidebar row returns to Write mode; only a click on its line scrolls the Read view.
- **The toolbar dock follows the standard dock curve, not the reference source.** The reference page's code could not be read, so the curve (peak 1.5, 84 px) is a re-implementation; its feel against the reference is for the owner to judge.
- **Option+Up and Option+Down are the only keyboard reorder (wave 5).** The sidebar's up and down arrow buttons are gone; dragging needs a pointer, so the chord on a focused row or heading is the keyboard path. With a search active it moves a row past the visible neighbour. The live-region announcement is proven to be rewritten each time, but VoiceOver re-reading an identical repeat has not been heard.
- **Scrollspy progress is not shown for research rows (wave 5).** The research panel has no per-row scroll position, so a Research line is idle, open or active but never partly filled; only Manuscript sections and fragments show how far they are scrolled. Lines are drawn in list view only, and the 24 px gutter (wave 7) holds lines up to 16 px.
- **Wave 5 was checked in Chromium only so far.** The sidebar rows, drag and chord reorder, scrollspy lines with magnify and progress, bounded popovers and the research panel spacing have browser and unit evidence; native WebKit has not been run for them. Not seen in native WebKit: hit-testing through the `display:contents` research sublists, and the cards view with a source and its PDF as one grid cell at 380 px or wider.
- **The hover-emphasis crawl can fail under load.** Two `tests/hover-emphasis.spec.ts` assertions (the Snapshot name dialog's Cancel scale and the dialog Save scale) read the 1.04 transition before it has applied on a busy machine; they fail the same way without any wave 5 change.
- **Floating mode needs margin to spare (wave 5, off by default).** The sidebar floats only if the left page margin is at least 222 px and the citation card only if the right margin is at least 312 px (190 + 2 x 16 and 280 + 2 x 16); otherwise the normal panels show. Page view above roughly 100 % zoom on a 1440 px window falls back, and so does a narrow window. Split view, destination pages (Settings, Comments, Bibliography) and Versions/History always use the normal panels. The Versions/History fallback has a rule but no spec of its own.
- **Focus after Cite here goes to the card's Page field (wave 7; wave 5 sent it to the footnote text field).** The card inserts the footnote, the page's auto-opened footnote field is not the focus target, and the card takes focus back one frame after Tiptap refocuses the editor. The Research panel still focuses its own footnote field. Cite here after a reload, before the editor was ever clicked, inserts at the start of the document, the same as the panel.
- **A project-tabs toggle can leave the floating top stale (wave 5).** The sidebar's `--float-top` and the card's top are re-resolved when the fit, the project, destination, entry, zoom or focus mode change; a change that only moves the workspace down (for example the project tabs bar) without changing the fit is not caught. The non-floating overlay sidebar probably has the same visibility-transition focus gap that floating fixed (untested).
- **Floating text has no backing (wave 5).** With no surface, sidebar text sits straight on the page background; contrast on `--paper` is 4.78:1 or better everywhere measured. The wave-4 1 px row separators are removed in floating mode only (T218).
- **Floating and the citation card were checked in Chromium only (wave 5).** Native WebKit (CSS zoom with `getBoundingClientRect`, pointer-modality animation, the card's layout) has not been run; the native-screens floating scenarios exist (`tests/native-screens.js`, captured at the end of every run at page zoom 75) but their real capture is V's, and was not run overnight. The card's Note form option mapping is duplicated from the Research panel and could drift.
- **No scroll fade while a menu or dialog is open (wave 5).** The fade on the Research panel, the citation card body and the floating sidebar's group list is switched off while a `.popover` (a Dropdown) or a `.modal-backdrop` is inside that region, so the edge snaps from faded to sharp as a menu opens. Any future overlay inside those regions with another class name would be faded. It relies on `:has`, and has not been seen in native WebKit.
- **The command palette lists app actions only (wave 5).** Editor-scope actions (formatting and the like) are not in it; it does not open without a project or while a modal dialog is up, and it closes when one opens. Chromium only.
- **Sidebar right-click menu (wave 5, T218).** It offers no menu on the bottom rows, and Move on a PDF row moves its source. In floating mode the row separators above the bottom rows are gone (docked keeps them). Chromium only. Type-to-filter menus and arrow-key tree navigation are out of scope until wave 6.

- **The unpinned hover sidebar still covers the left end of the format bar (wave 6).** When the sidebar is not pinned and you reveal it on hover, it floats above the workspace (`.sidebar-shell.is-overlay`, z-index 60) and covers the left end of the format bar and the top edge of the panes while shown. This is by design, since the overlay takes no layout space; pin the sidebar to keep the bar clear. Moving the overlay below the bar would fight the hover-reveal design and is a separate ask.
- **Each rail line is one more Tab stop (wave 6).** Every Manuscript, Fragments and Research group adds one Tab stop per item after its rows (the line rail). Tab from row to row is unchanged, but a long project means more Tab presses to cross the sidebar. Whether the extra stops are wanted is for the owner to say in real use.
- **Wave 7 is Chromium-first (CLAIMED).** The record is in [the wave-7 validation record](agent-work/2026-11-04-ui-wave-7/VALIDATION.md), with each native check marked pass, partial or not checked. Two native harness runs (T242) drive the app by commands. The second passed what it measured except one false failure from its own fixture, since corrected in the harness and not rerun. Real keys, typing, A4, split-pane measures, a visible scrollbar, drags, resize edges, a real quit and relaunch, and print or PDF export with a changed margin are not checked. Hover, window-resize and trackpad checks cannot be driven by the native harness and are owner hand checks.
- **Ruler (wave 7).** The Ruler switch lives in Page setup (owner's call: toolbar instead would push Focus mode and Prose check into More at 1440 px). A drag is not undoable with Cmd-Z. The ruler allows a 0.25 inch margin but the export panel's minimum is 0.5 inch. In continuous view the column is now 608 to 624 px wide (the old 8 px side padding is gone), so some lines rewrap. Page view and Word/PDF pagination still use different layout engines: no exact page parity. In native WebKit the caret line stays 5 px below the ruler when moved up by command (measured at zoom 0.75 to 1.15 and in two panes); real ArrowUp and ArrowDown, typing and moving down are not checked. A sliver of the line above shows under the ruler. The text column shrinks to nothing only below the 850 px minimum window.
- **Hierarchy not seen in Futura yet (wave 7).** Bold 700 headings, 500 unlit headings and dim 400 rows were checked in Chromium and by contrast numbers; the real look in macOS Futura is an owner check. The bold heading is slightly wider than the 500 one when it lights.
- **Split divider keeps its resting line on hover (wave 7).** By decision the three resize edges paint nothing on hover or drag; the divider's 1 px resting line stays because it is also the boundary between panes. The owner may want it gone on hover too.
- **CI flakes and open causes (wave 7, T238).** footnotes:48 and :134 failed once on the macOS-15 arm64 CI runner; the cause is unproven (an inferred race between Tiptap's deferred focus and the footnote field's autofocus-then-close; the restored `setPendingFocus` is identical to HEAD and a 150 ms rAF delay fails both builds). writing-aids:21 (focus dimming) is unexplained: locally it failed 14 of 20 runs on the current build and 5 of 10 on the older one with the caret staying in paragraph 2 after ArrowUp and Home. A node-selected footnote marker followed by Insert image drops the note (seen through a test only). Cause not found.
- **Wave 6 native evidence is partial.** The final check ran native WebKit captures at the fixed 1240 px window, light and dark, Plain and Color (palette, settings, Notes tab, 190 px sidebar, rail and preview cards, split view, toast beside the card, toolbar at rest and magnified, floating sidebar and card at page 50 %); hover, window resizing (1100/1600 px) and continuous 125/150 % stay Chromium-only or owner hand checks. Status stays CLAIMED until the owner confirms in the Mac app.

### (b) Known issues still open

- **Undo after deleting an empty footnote.** Backspace in an empty footnote that cites no source removes its numbered marker and its empty record together. Undo brings back the marker only, so that note has to be typed again.
- **PDF tables do not repeat the header row on continuation pages.** Word export marks the header row to repeat (confirmed by the owner in Word).
- **Zip header size cap.** A zip whose headers understate an entry's size is rejected only after that entry is unpacked, so a hostile file can cost memory first. The owner accepted this as a documented beta limit (Q003).
- **Citation after a block quotation.** A citation inserted after a block quotation can appear after the block rather than inside its final paragraph. Review the placement before export.
- **Comment anchors on identical passages.** Comments attach to quoted text. If two passages are identical, a comment may attach to the wrong one. If its passage is edited away, the comment is marked detached.
- **Zotero refresh gaps.** The refresh has no polling, no incremental updates, and no handling of items deleted in Zotero.
- **Large projects can slow writing aids.** Goal and citation summaries currently recompute across the project during renders.
- **Native print stopped.** A native WebKit print probe could not produce searchable Arabic: Arabic text containing the lam-alef ligature (for example السلام) could not be searched in its PDF with 26 of the 28 installed fonts tried. The probe exists only in a diagnostic build. The legacy PDF exporter remains the only PDF path, and exact native PDF preview and flowing per-page footnotes are cancelled (Q004, WP9/WP10). A bundled open-licence Arabic body font and a new probe are the recorded route to reconsider it.

- **Passage restore can be overwritten while saving (pre-existing, single pane too).** Restoring a passage writes the content and then waits for the save queue before remounting the editors; typing into the restored entry during that wait overwrites the restore. Recorded in the [later-fixes plan](agent-work/2026-10-25-later-fixes/PLAN.md) as L16.
- **The More menu stays open after clicking one of its rows.** Closing it needs Escape or a click outside; it is also the cause of the split-view menus browser-test flake (about 2 in 20 runs).
- **A PDF removed while it is open beside leaves the pane showing an alert.** Removing the PDF (or deleting its source) while it is open in the right pane shows "This PDF is not stored in this project." instead of closing the pane; `prune()` in `src/split.ts` checks that the source exists, not that it still has a PDF.
- **The PDF size label only counts up to KB.** A 25 MB PDF is shown as "25600.0 KB".
